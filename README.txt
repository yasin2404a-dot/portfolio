Files:

SETUP:
1. Open index.html in your browser.
2. The portfolio already includes your portrait extracted from the uploaded CV as assets/profile.jpg.
3. Replace the social URLs in index.html with your real Instagram, LinkedIn, Behance and GitHub links.
4. Replace project/post placeholders with your real images and project URLs.
5. Replace your-email@example.com with your email.

The portfolio includes:
- Soft light theme
- Black/dark theme
- Dark mode persistence using localStorage
- Smooth scrolling
- Scroll reveal animations
- Hover transitions
- Responsive mobile navigation
- Projects and posts sections
